Connect Google Drive

      
Paste the OAuth client id and secret from your Google Cloud project once (the steps are in docs/CLOUD_DEPLOYMENT.md, "Connect Google Drive"), press Connect, sign in on Google's page with your Google username and password, press Allow. The service creates the folder "SIKANDER AI Corpus" in your Drive and copies judgments into it every 30 minutes. Your Google password never reaches this service.
