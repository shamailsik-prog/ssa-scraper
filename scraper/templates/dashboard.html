Human login — PakistanLawSite

      
A server-side browser opens the login page and is streamed here. You type the username, password and any verification yourself; the service never sees or stores them. When the site shows you as logged in, press Complete: cookies and local storage are encrypted and saved to the chosen slot, and scraping continues headless inside that session. CAPTCHA or verification pages are never solved by code.
